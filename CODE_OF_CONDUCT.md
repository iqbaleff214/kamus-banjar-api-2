# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project is a community effort to preserve and digitize the Banjar language. We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates — regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Use welcoming and respectful language in all interactions
- Be constructive when giving or receiving feedback
- Acknowledge differing viewpoints and experiences
- Show empathy toward fellow community members
- Credit others' work appropriately

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Disrespectful, derogatory, or offensive language
- Personal attacks or inflammatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider unprofessional

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other community venue associated with this project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer:

**M. Iqbal Effendi** — iqbaleff214@gmail.com

Participants who violate this Code of Conduct may be warned, have their contributions removed, or be banned from the project at the maintainer's discretion.
